Privacy Policy for iHogu AI Sidebar

Effective Date: August 20, 2026
Last Updated: August 20, 2026

iHogu AI Sidebar (“iHogu”, “we”, “our”, or “the Extension”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Extension may access or process, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

By using iHogu AI Sidebar, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Access and Process

iHogu AI Sidebar may access or process the following information only when necessary to provide features requested by the user.

Website Content

When you actively use features such as webpage summarization, translation, explanation, rewriting, or contextual AI questions, the Extension may access:

  • Text visible on the current webpage
  • Text you select on a webpage
  • The title and URL of the current webpage
  • Other webpage content necessary to perform the specific AI-assisted action you request

The Extension does not continuously monitor your browsing activity.

Website content is accessed only when required to provide a user-requested feature.

User Input

We may process text, prompts, questions, or instructions that you voluntarily enter into the iHogu AI Sidebar.

Extension Settings

The Extension may store settings and preferences such as:

  • Interface preferences
  • Custom prompts
  • Feature settings
  • Language preferences
  • Other configuration information

These settings may be stored locally in your browser using Chrome extension storage.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by iHogu AI Sidebar is used only to provide and improve the functionality requested by the user, including:

  • Summarizing webpage content
  • Answering questions about webpage content
  • Translating text
  • Explaining selected content
  • Rewriting or improving text
  • Providing AI-assisted browsing features
  • Remembering user preferences and extension settings
  • Maintaining the reliability and security of the Extension

We do not use webpage content to create advertising profiles.

3. AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Some iHogu AI Sidebar features may use third-party artificial intelligence or infrastructure service providers.

When you actively request an AI-powered feature, the relevant webpage content, selected text, prompt, or other necessary information may be transmitted securely to these service providers so that your request can be processed.

Only information reasonably necessary to perform the requested feature is transmitted.

Third-party service providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

4. Information We Do Not Sell

iHogu does not sell, rent, or trade personal information or browsing data to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

We do not use user data for:

  • Targeted advertising
  • Creditworthiness assessment
  • Selling user profiles
  • Unrelated marketing
  • Tracking users across unrelated websites
  • Creating browsing-history profiles

5. Browsing History

iHogu AI Sidebar is designed to work with the webpage you are currently viewing.

We do not intentionally collect or maintain a history of all websites you visit.

Access to webpage content is used only as necessary to provide features that you actively request.

6. Permissions

iHogu AI Sidebar may request Chrome permissions necessary for its functionality.

These permissions may include:

activeTab

Allows the Extension to interact with the currently active webpage when you actively invoke the Extension.

scripting

Allows the Extension to access relevant content from the current webpage when necessary to perform a requested AI action.

contextMenus

Allows the Extension to provide right-click actions for selected text, such as summarizing, translating, explaining, or rewriting content.

sidePanel

Allows iHogu AI Sidebar to display its interface alongside the webpage you are viewing.

storage

Allows the Extension to save preferences, settings, and other configuration information required for normal operation.

Any permissions requested by the Extension are intended only to support its stated functionality.

7. Data Retention

We aim to minimize data retention.

Information sent for AI processing is used to fulfill the user’s requested action and is not intentionally retained by iHogu longer than reasonably necessary for providing, securing, or maintaining the service.

Locally stored extension preferences may remain in your browser until you change them, clear browser data, or uninstall the Extension.

Third-party service providers may apply their own data-retention policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by iHogu AI Sidebar.

Where information is transmitted to servers or third-party service providers, we use secure network connections such as HTTPS whenever applicable.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Sensitive Information

You should avoid submitting highly sensitive information through the Extension unless necessary.

This may include:

  • Passwords
  • Authentication credentials
  • Government identification numbers
  • Financial account information
  • Medical information
  • Other highly confidential personal information

iHogu AI Sidebar is not designed to intentionally collect this information.

10. Children’s Privacy

iHogu AI Sidebar is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law to independently consent to data processing.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your Choices

You control when iHogu AI Sidebar processes webpage content.

You may:

  • Choose whether to activate AI features
  • Avoid submitting particular webpage content
  • Delete locally stored extension settings
  • Disable the Extension
  • Uninstall the Extension at any time through Chrome

Uninstalling the Extension removes its access to your browser.

12. Chrome Web Store User Data

iHogu AI Sidebar handles user data only for purposes directly related to the Extension’s user-facing features.

We do not transfer or use user data for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the Extension’s functionality, except where required for security, legal compliance, or other purposes permitted by applicable law and Chrome Web Store policies.

13. International Data Processing

Depending on the infrastructure and AI service providers used by iHogu AI Sidebar, information necessary to process a request may be processed on servers located in countries or regions different from your own.

By using AI-powered features, you acknowledge that such processing may occur where necessary to provide the requested service.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • Changes to iHogu AI Sidebar
  • New features
  • Changes to service providers
  • Legal or regulatory requirements
  • Chrome Web Store policy requirements

When material changes are made, the “Last Updated” date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be revised.

15. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or iHogu AI Sidebar’s privacy practices, please contact us through the contact information published on the official iHogu website:

Website: https://ihogu.com
