iHogu AI Sidebar

iHogu AI Sidebar brings the official websites of leading AI assistants into Chrome’s Side Panel, so you can browse and work with AI side by side without constantly switching tabs. It uses your existing sign-in session on each provider’s website and does not require separate API keys.

One sidebar, many AI platforms

Switch between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Doubao, Grok, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Qwen, Kimi, Tencent Yuanbao, Mistral Vibe, and Poe from a single bilingual interface. You can also add a custom web-based AI service, including an internal company assistant, and configure its login page, brand color, and page selectors when needed.

Turn any webpage into useful AI context

iHogu helps you move information from the page you are reading into the AI conversation:

  • Select text and insert it into the current AI prompt or send it immediately.
  • Use the right-click menu or press Alt+Shift+A to send a selection to the sidebar.
  • Hover over a large image, Canvas, or SVG chart and attach it to the AI prompt.
  • Send the main article text and up to eight important images from the current page.
  • Optionally create a current-page-context.txt attachment or capture a full-page screenshot.
  • Avoid uploading the same page repeatedly within one AI conversation.

Press Alt+A whenever you want to open the sidebar.

Built for control and privacy

iHogu is local-first. Preferences are stored in Chrome, while prompts and attachments are passed directly to the official AI website you choose. The extension does not operate an AI proxy, does not call an AI API, and does not request access to your cookies or browsing history. Your provider account and chat history remain with that provider.

Optional webpage tools can be enabled or disabled individually, including the selection button, visual attachment tool, persistent page assistant, automatic page context, and full-page screenshots. The settings interface is available in English, Chinese, or the language selected in Chrome.

How it works

iHogu loads the selected provider’s official website inside Chrome’s Side Panel and reuses the login state from your current Chrome profile. Because AI websites can change their page structure, login flow, or embedding policy at any time, an individual provider may occasionally require an updated compatibility rule.
